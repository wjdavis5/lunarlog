## Full issue body
## Problem

A guardian's role is fixed at invitation time and cannot be changed afterwards. `SharingService` (`lib/domain/sharing/sharing_service.dart`) exposes `createInvite` (role chosen up front), `acceptInvite`, `listGuardians`, `revokeGuardian`, and invitation cancellation — but no "change this guardian's role" operation. Server-side there is `create_guardian_invitation`, `accept_guardian_invitation`, `revoke_guardian`, and `revoke_guardian_invitation`; there is no `update_guardian_role`.

The only way to move someone between roles today is to revoke them and send a fresh invitation they must redeem again on their device.

## Why it matters

Family arrangements change, and the roles in this app are explicitly modelled on family arrangements (`primary_guardian`, `co_parent`, `caregiver`, `viewer`). The changes are ordinary and predictable:

- A grandparent invited as `viewer` starts doing school-week care and now needs to log. Today that means revoking them and making them redeem a new link.
- A `co_parent` relationship changes and access should be narrowed to `viewer` rather than removed entirely. Today the only available action is the blunt one: full revocation.
- After ownership transfer to the young person, the parent's post-transfer role is chosen once during the transfer (`transfer_ownership_screen.dart` lets the arming parent pick co-manager or read-only). The young person, now the owner, has no way to adjust it later — which undercuts the "full autonomy" end state that flow is designed to reach.

Revoke-and-reinvite is not an acceptable substitute. It is user-visible churn, it requires the other person to be present and to redeem a link, and revocation has real side effects — the revoked guardian's local copy of the profile is tombstoned by the sync engine, and their unredeemed invitations are invalidated. A role adjustment should not destroy and rebuild a membership.

## Scope

- A `update_guardian_role(p_profile_id, p_target_user_id, p_new_role)` SECURITY DEFINER RPC, following the pattern already established by `revoke_guardian` and `revoke_guardian_invitation` (`supabase/migrations/20260906190000_revoke_guardian_invitation.sql`).
- A role ladder on who may change whom, consistent with the existing revoke ladder: the primary guardian may change anyone's role; a `co_parent` may change only `caregiver` and `viewer` roles; nobody may grant `primary_guardian` through this path.
- Client method on `SharingService` plus its Supabase implementation, with typed failures matching the existing `SharingFailure` taxonomy.
- UI in `ManageGuardiansScreen`: a role control on each guardian row, gated by the caller's own capability, with a confirmation that names what access is being added or taken away.
- The role change must reach the affected guardian's device and take effect there — including narrowing a `co_parent` to `viewer`, which must make their logging UI read-only on next sync.

## Design constraints

- **`primary_guardian` is not assignable here.** There is a single-accepted-primary-guardian constraint (R22 in the ownership-transfer plan) and transferring primary ownership is a separate, deliberately heavyweight flow with its own claim handshake. This RPC must not become a back door around it.
- A guardian must not be able to change **their own** role — in particular, not to escalate it.
- Narrowing a role must not orphan anything: entries the guardian previously logged keep their attribution unchanged.
- The client's read-only enforcement fails *open* for an unknown role by design (KTD8 of the family-sharing plan). A role narrowed to `viewer` must therefore land as a *known* role on the target device, not as an absence.
- pgTAP coverage to the standard the existing guardian RPC tests set, including the negative cases: self-escalation, co-parent attempting to demote the primary guardian, and granting `primary_guardian`.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The primary guardian can promote a `viewer` to `caregiver` and the change takes effect on that guardian's device after a sync, without any re-invitation.
- [ ] The primary guardian can narrow a `co_parent` to `viewer`; that device's day sheet becomes read-only with the correct view-only reason.
- [ ] A `co_parent` can adjust a `caregiver`/`viewer` role but cannot touch the primary guardian's role.
- [ ] No caller can change their own role.
- [ ] No path through this RPC grants `primary_guardian`.
- [ ] Attribution on previously-logged entries is unchanged by a role change.
- [ ] pgTAP proves each negative case is rejected server-side, not merely hidden in the UI.
